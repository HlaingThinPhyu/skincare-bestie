# User Feedback — Skincare Bestie

**Method:** In-person walkthrough + follow-up questions  
**Date:** 2026-07-24  
**Participant:** 1 real user (friend, late 20s, regular skincare routine)

---

## Session summary

Walked the user through the live app on Vercel. They selected their skin profile using the filter form and received a personalized routine. Session lasted ~10 minutes for multiple tries with different filters.

---

## What they said

**On the filter form:**
> "I liked that there are no text boxes — I never know what to type. Dropdowns made it feel more comfortable."

**On the results:**
> "The ingredient pills are nice but I don't know what half of them mean. Could there be a quick tooltip or explanation?"

**On product links:**
> "It actually shows me where to buy it,  that's the most useful part. I always end up on the wrong page on Amazon."

**On the disclaimer:**
> "I liked that it said to patch-test. Feels more trustworthy."

---

## Issues raised

| # | Feedback | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ingredient names not explained — need tooltips or short descriptions | Medium |
| 2 | No way to save or share the routine | Low |
| 3 | "Budget" dropdown steps feel uneven ($10-30 vs $60-100) | Low |
| 4 | Mobile layout on small screens cuts off product cards | Medium |

---

## What worked well

- Zero free-text inputs removed decision fatigue
- Step-by-step routine order made sense to the user
- Vendor links with prices were immediately actionable
- Dark/light mode toggle noticed and appreciated
- Language selection is good for varied people
---

## One-line takeaway

> "It's way simpler than reading a Reddit thread and google search for complete routine.I actually knew what to do after."
